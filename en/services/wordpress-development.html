Frequently Asked Questions

Why choose a custom WordPress theme over a marketplace theme?

Marketplace themes bundle thousands of unused features that destroy page speed and expose vulnerabilities. Custom themes load only necessary code, delivering 95+ PageSpeed scores and tailored security.

Can you integrate Saudi payment gateways like Mada and Apple Pay?

Yes. We natively integrate Moyasar, HyperPay, Tap, and PayTabs with full Apple Pay and Mada checkout support.

How do you handle bilingual Arabic and English websites?

We build native RTL and LTR support with dedicated Arabic font pairings (Cairo/Almarai) and zero layout shifts.
